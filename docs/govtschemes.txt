Government Schemes for Farmers: Odisha & India

Here are some government schemes available for farmers from both the Odisha Government and the Central Government of India:

**Odisha Government Schemes:**

* Krushak Assistance for Livelihood and Income Augmentation (KALIA): Provides financial, livelihood, cultivation support, and insurance assistance to small/marginal farmers and landless agricultural laborers.
* Bhoomihina Agriculturist Loan and Resources Augmentation Model (BALARAM): Focuses on providing crop loans to landless farmers/sharecroppers through Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).
* Mukhyamantri Krushi Udyog Yojana (MKUY): Encourages capital investment in agriculture by providing subsidies for setting up commercial agri-enterprises.
* Farm Mechanisation Schemes: Provides subsidies for purchasing agricultural implements and equipment (includes DBT for farm implements, Sourajalanidhi).
* Ama Krushi: Provides free, customized agricultural advice to enrolled farmers via phone.
* Jalanidhi: Focuses on harnessing water resources, potentially supporting irrigation via DBT.
* Schemes for Horticulture: Includes input subsidies for planting materials and promotion of vegetables/spices.
* Livestock Farming Schemes: Various schemes supporting poultry, duck, pig, goat farming, and silage making units.
* SAFAL (Simplified Application for Agricultural Loans): A common credit portal.
* GO-SUGAM: A portal related to agricultural activities.
* Krushak Odisha: Another portal/initiative for farmers.

**Central Government Schemes (Government of India) (Available across India, including Odisha):**

* Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN): Provides income support of ₹6,000 per year to eligible landholding farmer families.
* Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Affordable crop insurance scheme covering risks from pre-sowing to post-harvest.
* Pradhan Mantri Kisan MaanDhan Yojana (PM-KMY): Voluntary pension scheme for Small and Marginal Farmers.
* Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY): Aims to ensure access to irrigation.
* Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme & Modified Interest Subvention Scheme (MISS): Facilitates affordable short-term credit, including interest subvention.
* Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (AIF): Financing facility for post-harvest management infrastructure.
* National Mission on Edible Oils - Oil Palm (NMEO-OP) / National Mission on Oilseeds and Oil Palm (NMOOP): Focuses on increasing edible oil production.
* National Food Security Mission (NFSM): Aims to increase production of key crops through demonstrations, seed distribution, etc.
* Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization (SMAM): Promotes farm machinery use.
* Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY): Promotes organic farming through clusters.
* Soil Health Card Scheme: Provides soil nutrient status and fertilizer recommendations.
* National Horticulture Mission (NHM): Promotes horticulture development.
* National Bamboo Mission: Aims to increase bamboo plantation area.
* e-NAM (National Agriculture Market): Online trading platform for agricultural commodities.
* Kisan Call Centers (KCC): Provide agricultural advice over the phone.

Note: Eligibility, benefits, and application processes can change. Always check official government websites or local agricultural offices for the latest details.
